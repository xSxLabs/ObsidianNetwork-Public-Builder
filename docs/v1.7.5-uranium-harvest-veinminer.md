# v1.7.5 — Uranium mining enchantments & Veinminer

Both `obsidiannetwork:uranium_ore` and `obsidiannetwork:deepslate_uranium_ore` use vanilla-equivalent iron ore loot behavior. Normal harvest produces one raw uranium. Fortune I–III increase raw uranium through `minecraft:apply_bonus` / `minecraft:ore_drops`. Silk Touch produces **the exact ordinary or deepslate ore block** and does not receive the Fortune modifier.

Only **iron-tier or better pickaxes** qualify to mine for drops (`minecraft:mineable/pickaxe`, `minecraft:needs_iron_tool`, and `requiresCorrectToolForDrops()`). Hands, axes and shovels do not get ore drops, even when enchanted. This follows vanilla mining/drop semantics; it does not add an unbreakable block for incorrect tools.

Both ores are listed in `#c:ores` and `#c:ores/uranium` so Veinminer (MiraculixxT, default Ores group) discovers them; its default pickaxe and `needCorrectTool` settings apply. Different veinminer mods or custom server configs may need their own group settings.
