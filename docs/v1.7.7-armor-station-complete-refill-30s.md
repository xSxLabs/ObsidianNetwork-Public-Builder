# v1.7.7 — Power Armor station fully refills in <= 60 seconds

## Updated capacity and target
The original Power Armor item uses 4,000,000 FE (base).
Before this change the v1.7.7-only EnergyTool override doubled it to 8,000,000 FE.
The requested **further doubling** makes it **16,000,000 FE per equipped piece**,
or **64,000,000 FE per full four-piece suit**, without changing non-Power-Armor
EnergyTool item capacity. Existing stored FE is not reset.

On a real powered Armor Station, standing for at most **60 seconds at 20 TPS**
must completely refill all three independent resources **in parallel** from
zero, provided the attached external sources can deliver real FE and NutriPaste.

- **Armor FE:** the original 200,000 FE station energy buffer/input limit is
  upgraded to **400,000 FE**, and suit recharge transfers up to **400,000 FE
  every five ticks** (20 TPS / 5 ticks = 4 services/s; 1,600,000 FE/s maximum).
  With adequate incoming FE, 64M FE requires approximately 40 seconds, plus
  small runtime consumption and integrity costs; target <=60 seconds.
- **Integrity:** preserve 1000 HP across four pieces and the established
  10 HP/piece per five-tick service at 25 real FE per HP. All four pieces
  are fully repaired in approximately 25 seconds with real FE supply.
- **NutriPaste:** the helmet and station retain their established 1,000,000 mB
  capacity. The station takes only registered NutriPaste through the existing
  Fluid Cable and only debits paste actually inserted into the helmet.
  Armor Station destinations retain the existing 25,000 mB per five-tick
  fluid import fast path (approximately 10 seconds at 20 TPS).
- **Service order:** integrity, paste, then FE recharge, always server-side
  during the same existing five-tick station service. One player per station
  cycle, no extra ticker and no additional world scan.

## Safety / migration
- FE and NutriPaste are conserved: the Armor Station never fabricates either.
- The FE storage NBT key `v177_armor_station_fe` remains unchanged. Previously
  stored values are retained and are now clamped to 400k rather than 200k.
- All four existing armor items remain compatible. No item ID, upgrade, damage
  behavior, HUD integrity, J.A.R.V.I.S., or non-armor EnergyTool change.
- The original 30-second regression test filename is kept for workflow
  compatibility but now asserts the **60-second, 64M-FE** contract.
- With insufficient actual FE or NutriPaste supply, lower TPS, or interrupted
  player contact, real-world time may exceed 60 seconds; there is no free FE.
