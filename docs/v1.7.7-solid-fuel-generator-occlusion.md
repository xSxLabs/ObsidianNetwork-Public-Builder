# v1.7.7 — Solid Fuel Generator block-face occlusion fix

A placed Solid Fuel Generator must not remove the visible faces of the
adjacent wall or other solid blocks. As with the established Nutrinator
and Electric Water Pump correction, its BlockBehaviour.Properties get
a single noOcclusion() call.

The original block model and texture, collision and hit shapes, sound,
hardness, FE generation, fuel burn rate, inventory, redstone, GUI and
NeoForge capability are unchanged. This is a block-face culling fix,
not a transparent renderer or X-ray mode.

Source regression checks the exact one-registration edit. Full NeoForge
compilation and live in-game wall adjacency require the normal build
and server test.
