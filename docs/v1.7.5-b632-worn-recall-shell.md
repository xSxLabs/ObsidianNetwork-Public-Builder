Baseline: B631 green, user feedback: Recall textures STILL do not resemble
the real worn armor, despite the 24 exact source pixel sprites in B630.

The technical cause is in addition to the old synthetic atlas: B629 3D
cuboids are oversized (arms/legs 6.8–7.2px deep), upper and lower sections
overlap, and frontal chest plates extend outside the real torso silhouette.
Those proportions distort the correct surface art even if the PNG bytes match.

Correct those proportions on all 24 MK rigs using compatible worn silhouette
volumes and KEEP the exact pixel-identity UVs from B630/B631:
- two 5px-depth upper arms, two 5px-depth lower forearms, no Y-axis overlap
- thigh and boots separated at y=4 with 5px shell depth
- remove the double-lift front chest cuboids; front stays attached/closed
- exact worn torso (8.8px width x 12px height) and rear door halves
- one proper helmet faceplate
- stationary sidewalls remain hidden only during normal rear opening
- original 28 bone names, pivots, parent hierarchy, seven complete animation
  clips, all 24 texturing PNGs, and Java runtime files unchanged
- no actual player back-step, teleport or exit change; suit is placed in
  FRONT of player as before, through the trusted B117 transaction.
- recall remains exactly as green B631 and B629: flight head-first,
  brake, MK42-inspired 17 part assembly, speed and particles unmodified.

Limits: even precise worn sprite matching does not prove pixel-perfect
3D rendering from all camera angles. Final verification requires in-game
video, side/back angles and faceplate visuals.
